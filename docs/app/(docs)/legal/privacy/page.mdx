# SudoBot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 14, 2026<br />
**Last updated:** August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the OSN Developers Team, referred to as “OSN,” “we,” “us,” or “our,” collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal data when you use the official hosted version of SudoBot, its official website, dashboard, verification pages, support services, and related features, collectively referred to as the “Service.”

SudoBot is a free and open-source Discord security and moderation bot. We process data only to provide, secure, maintain, and support the Service. We do not sell personal data, rent it to others, use it for targeted advertising, for AI/ML/LLM training or to build profiles for purposes unrelated to the bot's functionality itself.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies only to the official SudoBot services operated by OSN.

It does not apply to Discord itself, third-party websites or services, or independently hosted copies, forks, or modified versions of SudoBot. Anyone who operates a self-hosted or modified instance is responsible for that instance and should provide their own privacy notice. Public access to SudoBot’s source code does not make data from the official hosted Service public.

Discord server owners and administrators choose whether to install SudoBot and which features to enable. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, a server owner or administrator may be independently responsible for how SudoBot is configured and used in their community.

## 2. Data We Process

The data SudoBot processes depends on the features enabled by a server administrator and how you interact with the Service.

### 2.1 Discord account and server data

We may process Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, avatars, account creation information made available by Discord, server membership, roles, permissions, and other Discord profile or member information required by enabled features.

We may also process Discord server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, webhook IDs, invite information, permission settings, and server configuration.

### 2.2 Messages, commands, and interaction data

When required by an enabled feature, SudoBot may process message content, edited or deleted messages, commands, command options, attachments, images, links, reactions, embeds, and related metadata such as the author, channel, server, and timestamp.

Most message content is processed in real time and is not stored after processing unless an enabled feature needs to retain it. Examples include scheduled messages, snippets, moderation evidence, infraction records, reports, audit logs, or other content that a server administrator has configured SudoBot to retain.

### 2.3 Moderation and security data

We may process and retain data needed to detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to spam, raids, abuse, fraud, ban evasion, alternate accounts, malicious links, unsafe content, or violations of a server’s rules.

This may include warnings, reports, moderation actions, infraction history, reasons supplied by moderators, moderator and affected-user IDs, timestamps, role changes, bans, mutes, timeouts, message counts, risk signals, matched rules, attachments, and related evidence.

### 2.4 Verification and anti-abuse data

If a server enables member verification, VPN or proxy detection, CAPTCHA checks, or alternate-account detection, we may process

- Your IP address and an indication of whether it appears to use a VPN, proxy, hosting provider, or other risk-associated network
- CAPTCHA tokens and verification results
- Discord OAuth information authorized by you, such as your Discord identity and the list of servers available through the approved OAuth scope
- Verification session tokens, status, attempts, and timestamps
- Device or browser fingerprints used to identify likely alternate accounts

All of these information are collected outside of Discord, and you have the option to stop at any moment.

Device or browser fingerprints may be derived from characteristics such as browser user agent, screen properties, language, timezone, canvas rendering, WebGL rendering, audio characteristics, installed fonts, CPU information, media-device characteristics, and ad-blocking behavior. These signals are used only for verification, fraud prevention, and alternate-account detection.

We do not receive your Discord password.

### 2.5 Website and account data

If you create or use an account on an official SudoBot website or dashboard, we may process your username, Discord ID, connected GitHub or Google account ID if you choose to connect one, authentication and recovery records, session tokens, account settings, and the servers you are authorized to manage.

### 2.6 Technical, diagnostic, and support data

We may process service logs, error reports, request timestamps, feature events, diagnostic information, and support communications when needed to operate, troubleshoot, protect, or support the Service.

## 3. Where Data Comes From

We receive data

- From Discord through its API and OAuth services
- Directly from you when you use commands, complete verification, use a website or dashboard, or contact support
- From Discord server administrators and moderators when they configure SudoBot or create moderation records
- From security and verification providers when an enabled feature checks a CAPTCHA, IP address, or other risk signal
- Automatically from your browser or device when you use an official verification or web page

## 4. How We Use Data

We use personal data only when reasonably necessary to

- Provide commands, automation, moderation, logging, verification, scheduling, role management, and other requested SudoBot features
- Apply the settings and security rules selected by Discord server administrators
- Detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to spam, raids, fraud, abuse, ban evasion, alternate accounts, malicious content, and threats to users or the Service
- Create and maintain moderation, audit, and security records
- Authenticate users and determine which servers they are authorized to manage
- Operate, maintain, debug, improve the reliability of, and secure the Service
- Respond to support requests and data-rights requests
- Enforce our Terms of Service and comply with applicable law

We do not use personal data for advertising, sell it, rent it, or use it to train general-purpose artificial intelligence models.

## 5. Automated Moderation and Decisions

SudoBot can automatically analyze activity and take actions selected by a Discord server’s administrators. Depending on configuration, the Service may flag or delete content, deny verification, assign or remove roles, issue a warning, mute or time out a member, kick or ban an account, or alert server moderators.

Automated signals can be incorrect. If you want to contest a moderation action, contact the moderators or administrators of the Discord server where the action occurred. OSN can address operation of the Service but generally cannot override a server administrator’s moderation decision.

Where required by applicable law, you may also contact us about a decision based solely on automated processing that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

## 6. Legal Bases for Processing

If European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or similar data-protection law applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases

- Performance of a contract or steps requested before entering a contract, when processing is necessary to provide a feature you request
- Legitimate interests, including operating the Service, protecting Discord communities, preventing abuse and fraud, securing accounts and infrastructure, and improving reliability
- Compliance with a legal obligation
- Consent, when applicable law requires it and we specifically request it

You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. We will assess the request against our legal and security obligations.

## 7. How We Disclose Data

We disclose data only as needed for the purposes described in this Policy.

### 7.1 Discord

SudoBot operates through Discord. Data and moderation actions may be sent to Discord or displayed to authorized users within a Discord server.

### 7.2 Service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, databases, backups, security, CAPTCHA verification, IP-risk checks, authentication, and error monitoring. They may process data only to provide contracted services to us and must protect it appropriately.

Depending on which features are enabled, providers may include

- Google Perspective API, which may receive message text for toxicity and safety classification when AI-based auto-moderation is enabled
- Google reCAPTCHA or Cloudflare Turnstile, which may receive CAPTCHA tokens, IP addresses, and browser or device information during verification
- A network-risk assessment service provider, which may receive an IP address when VPN or proxy checking is enabled
- Discord, GitHub, or Google when you choose to authenticate or connect an account through OAuth
- Our hosting, database, backup, and infrastructure providers

We will provide an up-to-date list of material service providers upon request.

### 7.3 Server administrators and moderators

Moderation, verification, audit, and security data may be visible to authorized administrators or moderators of the relevant Discord server. They are responsible for their own access to and use of that data.

### 7.4 Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose data when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law, valid legal process, or necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, Discord communities, OSN, or the public.

### 7.5 Organizational changes

If responsibility for the Service is transferred through a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, relevant data may be transferred subject to this Policy and applicable law.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the feature, security purpose, legal obligation, or dispute for which it was collected.

Our recommended standard retention periods are

| Data category | Standard retention |
| --- | --- |
| Message content processed only for real-time rules | Deleted after processing and ordinarily not persistently stored |
| Verification session tokens and pending verification records | Until completed or expired, then deleted within 24 hours |
| Verification IP addresses and detailed attempt logs | Up to 30 days |
| Device or browser fingerprints used for alternate-account detection | Up to 180 days from the most recent verification or security event |
| Operational and error logs | Up to 30 days |
| Scheduled actions, reminders, and temporary queues | Until the action completes, is canceled, or expires, plus up to 30 days for recovery and debugging |
| Moderation, infraction, and security records | Up to 12 months from the most recent related activity, unless deleted earlier by an authorized administrator or retained longer for an active investigation |
| Server configuration and feature records | While SudoBot remains installed, then deleted or de-identified within 30 days after removal |
| Official website or dashboard account data | While the account remains active, then deleted or de-identified within 30 days after an authenticated deletion request |
| Backups | Removed through the normal backup cycle within 30 additional days |
| Support and data-rights correspondence | Up to 24 months after the matter is closed |

Some data may be retained longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to abuse, enforce agreements, resolve disputes, protect the Service, or establish or defend legal claims. When practical, we restrict further use of data subject to a legal or security hold.

A Discord server administrator may configure shorter retention periods or delete certain server records sooner where the Service supports it.

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data and the risks of processing. These may include access controls, authentication, encryption in transit, credential and secret management, backups, monitoring, software updates, and limiting access to people who need it to operate or support the Service.

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If we become aware of a data breach, we will investigate and provide notifications when required by applicable law.

## 10. International Data Transfers

OSN and its service providers may process data in countries other than your own. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we use legally recognized safeguards for international transfers and take reasonable steps to require service providers to protect the data.

You may contact us for more information about safeguards applicable to your data.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to

- Request access to personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of data
- Request restriction of processing
- Object to certain processing
- Request a portable copy of certain data
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Appeal or complain about how a request was handled
- Lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority

To submit a request, email **rakinar2@onesoftnet.eu.org** and include your Discord user ID, the relevant Discord server ID if applicable, and the type of request. Do not send your password or authentication token.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. We may deny or limit a request when permitted by law, including when necessary to protect other users, prevent fraud or abuse, preserve evidence, or respect the rights of a Discord server administrator or another person.

Some server-specific data is controlled or maintained by the server’s administrators. We may direct you to the relevant server administrator when they are best positioned to address the request.

Authorized agents may submit requests where applicable law permits. We may require proof of authorization and identity.

## 12. United States State Privacy Disclosures

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising and do not use sensitive personal data to infer characteristics for advertising or unrelated profiling.

If a United States state privacy law applies to us and to your data, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of covered personal data and may appeal a denied request. We will not unlawfully discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

## 13. Children’s Privacy

SudoBot is designed for use through Discord and is not directed to children below the minimum age required by Discord or applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child who is not permitted to use Discord.

If you believe a child has provided data through the Service in violation of applicable age requirements, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

## 14. Responsibilities of Discord Server Administrators

Server owners and administrators who install or configure SudoBot should

- Inform their members that SudoBot is used and provide access to this Policy
- Enable only features and permissions reasonably necessary for their community
- Avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through commands, moderation reasons, reports, or saved content
- Limit access to SudoBot logs and records to trusted moderators
- Provide a reasonable way for members to ask questions or appeal moderation actions
- Comply with applicable privacy, employment, education, and communications laws

## 15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to SudoBot, our practices, or applicable law. We will post the updated Policy with a new “Last updated” date.

If a change materially affects how previously collected personal data is used or disclosed, we will provide advance notice through an appropriate channel when reasonably practicable and obtain consent when required by law.
